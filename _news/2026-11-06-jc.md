We contributed to two recent reviews on gene regulation. Selin wrote a [Journal Club piece](https://rdcu.be/gYkevUNrDpba) to *Nature Reviews Genetics* on the landmark transchromosomic experiment by [Wilson et al, 2008](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1160930), demonstrating the role of *cis* sequence over the *trans* cell environment in determining gene regulation - a great chance to reflect on how sequence-to-function modeling can address this question! [On the Genomics x AI blog](https://genomicsxai.github.io/blogs/2026-017/), Selin co-led a review on computational models for gene regulation that learn cell context-dependence. We provide a framework for thinking about models of sequence only, cell state only, and joint models that capture both, along with design considerations and future directions!
