Selin contributed a short [Journal Club piece](https://rdcu.be/gYkevUNrDpba) to *Nature Reviews Genetics* on the landmark transchromosomic experiment by [Wilson et al, 2008](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1160930), demonstrating the role of *cis* sequence over the *trans* cell environment in determining gene regulation. A great chance to reflect on how sequence-to-function modeling can address this question!
