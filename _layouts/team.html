We are looking for curious, collaborative, creative scientists to join our team!

Graduate students

Our group is part of the Molecular Genetics department at UofT, which has a central admission committee and a rotation system. Graduate students interested in joining the lab must first be accepted in Molecular Genetics. First-year students should email Selin to discuss rotation opportunities.

Summer students for 2027

Summer students are exclusively selected from successful applicants to the Research Training Centre (RTC) at the Lunenfeld-Tanenbaum Research Institute. Keep an eye out for applications in early 2027, due on February 28th of each year.
